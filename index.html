Partner opportunity · Commission-only

Know the massage industry? Let's build Booked Up together.

Booked Up is a booking app for massage therapists, chair massage pros, barbers and similar providers. I'm looking for an industry insider to bring it to the people who need it.

Apply to partner

Who I'm looking for

I'm looking for a business-minded self-starter who has started a business before and knows this industry from the inside, such as an experienced massage therapist or someone stepping back from hands-on work. Most of all, I want someone who comes up with creative, low-cost ways to land clients and acts on them without waiting to be told.

Must-haves

  • Experience starting and running your own business
  • An initiator: you come up with low-cost ways to find clients (outreach, blogging, posting, partnerships) and follow through on them
  • Real relationships with therapists, spas, barbers, other providers, or companies that buy wellness services
  • Comfort with sales: making calls and asking for the meeting
  • Reliability: you show up and follow through
  • Basic tech comfort, enough to run a demo and walk a provider through setup

Preferred

  • An active social media presence you use to do business: posting, blogging and outreach that bring in real customers. Results matter far more than follower count.

You'd work as an independent contractor, not an employee.

What you do, and what I do

You

Marketing, sales, onboarding and first-line support for the accounts you bring in.

Me

The technology and the product: building, hosting, fixing and improving Booked Up.

Booked Up works for providers at a spa or office, or at the client's own site, and prices at $17 per person per month, with small discounts for larger teams.

How you get paid

  • 50% of collected revenue from the accounts you bring in. It's a revenue share, not ownership or equity in the company.
  • Residuals for up to 10 years per account, as long as that account keeps paying. You're paid only on money actually collected.
  • Tracked and paid through Rewardful by direct deposit, monthly, by the 15th of each month. Each commission is held 30 days after the customer pays so refunds can clear, then paid in the next monthly payout.
  • No salary. I may offer an optional marketing budget when I have it available. It's at my discretion and approved in advance.
Please read: Earnings depend on your results and are not guaranteed. This is a commission-only partnership opportunity, not a job offer, and there is no promise of any income. Final terms will be set in a written agreement, which I recommend an attorney review.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a job?

No. It's a commission-only partnership. You'd be an independent contractor, not an employee, and there is no salary.

How much will I earn?

I can't promise an amount. You receive 50% of the revenue collected from the accounts you bring in, so your earnings depend on your results. Booked Up is $17 per person per month, with small discounts for larger teams.

Do I get equity in the company?

No. This is a revenue share, not ownership or equity.

How long do residuals last?

Up to 10 years per account, as long as that account keeps paying. Details such as what happens if you stop actively working with Booked Up will be set in the written agreement.

When and how am I paid?

Monthly, by the 15th, by direct deposit through Rewardful. Each commission is held for 30 days after the customer pays so refunds can clear, then included in the next monthly payout.

What happens after the 90-day trial?

We review the results together. The goals are 30 demos booked and 10 paying providers signed, with 3 paying providers as the minimum to continue. Then we decide together whether to keep going.
